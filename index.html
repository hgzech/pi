Digits

Blank

th, w, h, y and every vowel carry no digit. A word with one digit-bearing consonant doubles it: Athena 22, Ivy 88, Owl 55, Earth 44.

How a stage works

Each stage is four digits. The first pair names an active peg, the second a passive one, and you picture the first doing something to the second in the next spot of your palace. Write the locus and the action straight into the sentence — "On my bed in Leiden, a turtle looks through a telescope" — then lock it in and the position advances. The two pegs in the sentence are editable too, so you can reword one or write your own; anything you type is kept as a chip for that bucket from then on.

Chips

Tap a word to choose it, ? to see what it is. A red mark lists the stages where you already used that word. Anything you pick rises to the top of that bucket next time the same number comes round, so your favourites settle out on their own. Long buckets show the first 40; filtering searches all of them.

Peg lists

The app reads pegs/00.md through pegs/99.md next to this page. Buckets you haven't generated yet fall back to a text field, so you can still work. Served over file:// the browser blocks those reads — use a local server or GitHub Pages.

Storage

Everything lives in this browser only. Export now and then, especially before clearing site data or switching phones.
